# Civilian robots, by 2040

The city uses a mixed fleet of recognizable commercial machines. Newer units improve autonomy, manipulation, coordination, reliability and battery management. Cheap units retain cheap bodies; old fleets remain in service alongside premium replacements. Most street activity belongs to wheeled delivery and cleaning machines. Bipeds work where stairs, doors and existing tools justify their shape. Quadrupeds suit inspection routes and occasional consumer use. Aircraft use delivery corridors and designated pads.

These are fictional products. Use several engineering references per category and create original shell profiles, limb proportions, joint covers, sensor arrangements and cargo openings. Do not reproduce a manufacturer's complete silhouette, branding or signature finish. Model names are production vocabulary, not floating HUD labels.

Personality comes from operation: a repeated pause before a kerb, a grip check, a rattling bearing, a replacement panel or different firmware recovery time. Damage and responses should remain heavy and restrained. Ordinary robots use task cancellation, safety stops and retreat policies rather than theatrical panic. Neutral hulls participate in combat physics without earning hostile kills or accuracy credit.

## Military relationship

The squad belongs to the expensive end of the same industry. ANCHOR and BREECH have hardened working-biped ancestry; NEEDLE prioritizes precise sensing and aiming over protection; ROOK and SPINDLE carry reinforced joints, substantial power systems and recoil-control hardware. Their exceptional capability has weight, cooling, maintenance and violent duty-cycle costs. Bracing, stagger and weapon handling express those limits.

## Sound

Delivery machines: tyre texture, small electric motor whine, lid latches and sparse delivery chimes. Aircraft: rotors. Bipeds: joint motors, cooling and foot impacts. Scrubbers: pumps and brushes. Keep civilian motors below combat sounds, with spatial panning and distance attenuation. Quieter premium models should be distinguished through motion and packaging as well as sound.

CART and CRATE are playable in the city district. CRATE uses a 1.12 × 1.34 m chassis, 1.26 m high, with 115 kg mass, 80 integrity and a 1.2 m/s cruise. Nearby combat cancels collection, closes the compartment and reduces its withdrawal speed to 0.85 m/s. Its motor pitch is lower than CART's, within the same quiet civilian mix.
