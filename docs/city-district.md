# City district

A city should feel large through connected streets, repeated architecture, movement and responses. Playable missions can occupy a small district in real-world dimensions. The district is now 234 × 147 metres (about 3.44 hectares), almost three times the original area. The central shops connect to a western parcel neighbourhood, a southern station forecourt and an eastern canal court. Roads continue beyond the playable area into adjacent skyline blocks. It is an activity sandbox, without a campaign objective or enemy wave controller.

## Play

Choose **CITY DISTRICT** in either combat-floor selector. The squad begins selected at the intersection. Twenty CARTs make local and cross-town deliveries; five CRATE parcel cabinets serve the depot, station hall, library and apartments. Three KITE parcel quadrotors fly between the western depot and marked receivers at the station and canal court. Pan east to the water or west to the parcel yard. Walk alongside the fleet, occupy a delivery lane or landing pad, fire nearby or throw a grenade to see its responses. Ordinary squad controls, specialist loadouts and sniping remain available. **Shift+R / Reset district** restores all three civilian models, cargo visits and the squad.

KITE has exposed rotor arms, four motors, skids and a secured parcel cradle. It cruises at 6 m/s, with an 18 kg compound physical hull and 24 integrity. Each aircraft has its own depot pad and 18, 21 or 24 m corridor, above the tallest roof equipment. Approaches are vertical. A squad member, delivery cart, loose prop or other aircraft occupying the pad makes it hover above collection height. Five-second stops unload at the destination or reload at the depot. One aircraft starts in flight, with the others departing on staggered schedules. A disturbance near the aircraft, shot trajectory or destination cancels collection: it climbs vertically before crossing back to its depot, then waits for quiet. Hits remove lift and unlock tumbling physics; survivors strand, and dead aircraft remain physical targets. Aircraft hits earn no hostile kill or accuracy credit. Ground blasts use three-dimensional distance and solid occlusion, so high aircraft remain outside the blast radius.

`DeliveryPad` and `FlightRoute` in `src/game/city.ts` define the reusable pad locations, endpoint IDs, corridor waypoints, altitude and livery. `ParcelFlights` in `src/game/aircraft.ts` drives finite thrust along these routes without invoking ground pathfinding. `kiteBody`, `makeDeliveryPad` and `KiteFleet` in `src/render/kites.ts` provide reusable geometry, paint and instanced moving parts. Compound colliders include the spinning rotor discs, body, cargo and skids; aim guides and weapon hits resolve every collider to its neutral owner. Rotor sound is synthesized in Web Audio, limited to three spatial voices, and shares **Civilian motors** with CART and CRATE. Pause, reset, mute and range changes release its voices.

CART is a small commercial six-wheeler: a roughly 0.8 m-wide, 0.94 m-long hull, 0.62 m high, with a mast. Prototype tuning uses 35 kg, 36 integrity and 1.6 m/s cruising speed. A CART slows for turns, yields to nearby hulls and observes alternating crossing signals. Stops at shops and homes open its lid. Blocked routes can reverse along their existing loop rather than repeatedly invoking squad pathfinding.

CRATE is a recognizable parcel-delivery cabinet on six larger wheels, with a low battery base and three stacked, independently hinged compartments. It is 1.12 m wide, 1.34 m long and 1.26 m high, with 115 kg mass and 80 integrity. Its wider clearance, 1.2 m/s cruise and slower turns distinguish it from a scaled CART. Stops last four to six seconds; it faces the collection building and rotates through compartments over successive visits. Gunfire closes the open compartment, cancels collection and prompts a slow 0.85 m/s withdrawal. Its larger mass resists bullet pressure, while grenades can still topple it. The two fleets yield to one another and share impact recovery and neutral scoring rules.

Nearby shots interrupt deliveries. Civilian units brake briefly and choose a direction away from the disturbance along their known route. Nearby shop shutters close. Local service resumes after a quiet period; unaffected streets keep working. Bullet hits lift and tumble the lightweight CART; exposed grenades launch it several metres. Steering and upright rotation control disengage during impacts. A surviving hull resumes its route only after it settles on its wheels with a clear route connection; tipped hulls remain stranded. Disabled hulls can still be knocked around by further fire or explosions. Bullets and exposed blasts hit neutral hulls, but civilian damage contributes no hostile accuracy, grenade-hit or kill credit. Tall building hulls block shots and blast exposure. Hovering either civilian model explicitly aims at its hull.

The 14 × 116 m canal is a shallow ornamental watercourse enclosed by low stone banks. Two broad crossings carry asphalt lanes and sidewalks through the canal at z = 0 and z = 48; railings and contrasting sidewalk strips show their structure. Ground orders route around the water and use the crossings. Navigation masks cover only the wet sections and do not create invisible physical walls: bullets and launched hulls can cross the water. A civilian hull that lands in a wet section becomes stranded behind the banks, with no invented damage or hostile score. It stays physical and can be pushed back onto dry ground. Bridge decks remain dry. Water ripples are subtle and opaque, with normal lighting and depth writes.

New reusable depot, civic-hall and pump-house buildings give the extensions distinct uses: roller doors and rooflights in the parcel yard, a clock and entrance canopy at the station and library, and exposed pipework on the quay. Forecourt paving, canal furniture and the remaining apartments connect these landmarks to everyday activity. No floating names are added.

Motor whirrs are synthesized locally with two quiet oscillator components per moving voice. The pause menu has an independent **Civilian motors** mix slider, saved with other preferences; it defaults to 40% of the original motor level and can silence them without changing weapon sounds. At most six nearby civilian motors play, with speed-dependent pitch, panning and distance attenuation. CRATE has a lower motor pitch than CART. Pause, floor changes, resets and disabled/stopped/tumbling units release voices. There are no new downloaded samples or runtime services.

## Reusable construction

- `src/game/city.ts` owns district extents, forecourt paving, the building kit, transforms, road/sidewalk segments, crossing junctions, street-furniture footprints, water sections and delivery routes. It contains no renderer or physics dependencies. `buildingSolid()` derives collision and navigation from the same dimensions and quarter-turn transform used to place the visual prefab. `waterSolids()` derives banks, railings and navigation-only wet masks from a canal's crossings. Roads, backdrop scenery and lighting extents use authored dimensions rather than the old block size.
- `src/render/city.ts` constructs shops, apartments, offices, workshops, depots, civic halls and pump houses from reusable window bays, doors, awnings, roof equipment and shutters. Exterior facade panels surround actual openings, without redundant interior faces; recessed panes and frame rings replace overlapping boxes and duplicate storefront/door windows. Rigid geometry is batched by material within each building; adjacent scenery shares materials and batches across buildings. A building uses multisample coverage to fade when it hides selected squad members, retaining depth writes and avoiding transparent facade sorting artifacts (with ordinary blending when antialiasing is unavailable); its physical hull remains solid. Scope mode restores full opacity. Adjacent buildings are solid scenery beyond the movement boundary.
- `src/render/streets.ts` joins road strips into a disjoint asphalt union, then cuts sidewalks and plaza paving around it. Junctions share the ordinary road height; bridge supports stay below the same continuous surface. Lane paint skips crossing carriageways. Geometry is batched with the other ground details.
- `src/game/navigation.ts` stores static inflated occupancy once per level. Dynamic props and live practice targets are rasterized into reusable search storage per order. A binary heap, octile heuristic and local endpoint search replace full-grid endpoint scans and linear frontier selection. Clear orders return immediately. Failed arrival recovery retries at most twice a second, and robots skip visible corners after local yielding carries them past a turn.
- `src/game/civilians.ts` owns CART and CRATE bodies, chassis tuning, delivery state, crossing policy, mutual yielding, local alerts and damage. It is separate from armed actors and arena AI. Each route selects a chassis, fleet count and service-stop IDs rather than requiring per-instance scripts.
- `src/render/carts.ts` instances each chassis's rigid components, six wheels, lights, CART lids and CRATE compartment doors. More instances do not add one draw call per wheel or compartment. Physics transforms interpolate normally, including tumbling and disabled hull rotation. Both fleets release instance buffers on reset or floor changes.
- `src/render/water.ts` constructs canals of different lengths with data-defined crossings, banks and railings. Its small water shader animates highlights using simulation time, so pause also stops the water. It requires no downloaded textures or reflection render pass.
- `src/render/primitives.ts` provides simple building blocks and rigid-part batching usable by later civilian and environment prefabs.

The district keeps building interiors closed. Facades are real solids, not traversable painted scenery. Pavement lips and bridge decks are shallow visual dressing on a shared collision floor; tall fixtures, benches, planters, banks, railings and building hulls have navigation/collision footprints. Fleet motion does not use the squad's grid search. Temporary live civilian hulls yield rather than becoming permanent pathfinding obstacles.

## Extending toward missions

Author additional districts with the same prefab and footprint vocabulary. Put objectives, entrances and civilian activity on district data rather than inside a renderer. Vary routes, chassis, fleet density, service stops, finishes, building transforms, water crossings and delivery timing. Use a few active adjacent streets to suggest the larger city; do not simulate kilometres of invisible traffic.

Place skyline buildings in parcels between continuing streets. Keep their hulls off the carriageways and sidewalks, and allow space for awnings and roof trim between neighbours. The district's northern scenery uses two rows; its western pairs face their nearest cross streets and now belong to the playable neighbourhood.

Later work can add road vehicles, charging docks and other civilian chassis. Keep their policies local and bounded: near combat, cancel tasks and suspend affected services; elsewhere, maintain normal activity. Reopening shops, maintenance visits and rerouted deliveries should show recovery after the player's activity. Avoid mission-status prose hovering over every machine.

`window.amortization2.inspect().city` reports positions, route destinations, model, active compartment, deliveries, distance travelled, state, crossing phase and closed shops for QA. Its `kites` list includes aircraft altitude, velocity, parcel load and rotor speed. The tactical camera stays above the air corridors at close zoom so aircraft remain visible and pickable. The sandbox is deterministic at the fixed simulation timestep. Inspectors do not issue movement or damage commands.
