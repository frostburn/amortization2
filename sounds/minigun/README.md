# Minigun edits

These derived audio files, including the synthesized shot bass, are released under **CC0 1.0**: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/ . Original recordings by qubodup are also CC0; their manifest is unchanged in `../_readme_and_license.txt`.

The source mapping, timings and runtime sequencing are documented in [`../../docs/minigun.md`](../../docs/minigun.md). Rebuild with `python scripts/build-minigun-sounds.py` (FFmpeg, NumPy and SciPy). The checked-in WAVs work without those editing tools. `demo.wav` is an audition; it is not imported by the game.
